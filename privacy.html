Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Doreh, Inc. (“Doreh,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information through doreh.com and related pages (the “Site”). It also covers Doreh’s text messages, described in section 4. Doreh products will have their own privacy notice when they become available.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Early access requests: your email address and the role you select (for example, host, planner, or vendor).
  • Contact form: your name, email address, subject, and message.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical information sent by your browser, such as IP address, approximate country derived from your IP address, browser type, the page that referred you, and the date and time of your visit.
  • Aggregate visit statistics through a privacy-focused analytics service that does not use cookies and does not identify you.

We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking on the Site.

2. How we use information

  • to respond to your messages and requests;
  • to contact you about early access, launch, and related updates;
  • to understand how many people visit the Site and improve it;
  • to protect the Site against spam, fraud, and abuse (for example, limiting how often a form can be submitted from one network address); and
  • to comply with law and enforce our terms.

3. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only:

  • with service providers that host the Site, store form submissions, and provide analytics on our behalf, under obligations to protect it (for example, our hosting and storage provider, Vercel, Inc.);
  • if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Doreh or others; and
  • in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

4. Text messages

Numbers you give us

If you enter your mobile number to receive a sign-in code, or turn on text updates for an event, we use your number only to send you those messages.

Numbers a host gives us

When a host invites you to an event, they may give us your mobile number, and you may receive texts from that host sent through Doreh. We use it only to deliver that host’s invitation and updates about that event: RSVP confirmations, reminders, day-of changes, and thank-you notes. We do not use it to market Doreh to you. Every text links to your settings, where you can choose all texts, the invitation only, or no texts. The host is responsible for having your permission to share your number with us for their event. We rely on the host’s confirmation, and we keep records of host confirmations, opt-ins, and opt-outs.

No sharing for marketing

We do not sell or share your mobile number, or your text message opt-in data and consent, with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. We share your number only with the messaging provider that delivers our texts, under obligations to protect it, and as required by law.

Frequency, cost, and opting out

Message frequency varies by event, plus one message for each sign-in code you request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, or email support@doreh.com. Reply STOP at any time to stop all texts from Doreh; this applies to every host on Doreh and takes effect immediately. You can also ask us to stop by emailing us or in any other reasonable way, and we will honor that request promptly and within 10 business days. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. We keep a record of your opt-out so that we can honor it. See doreh.com/sms for details.

5. Retention

We keep early access and contact information until Doreh launches and you have had the opportunity to create an account or opt out, or until you ask us to delete it, unless we need to keep it longer to comply with law. Records used only to limit spam are kept for a short period.

6. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your information, or to stop sending you updates, at any time. Depending on where you live (for example, California or the European Economic Area), you may have additional rights, including the right to data portability and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising your rights. We will respond as required by applicable law. Every update email will include a way to unsubscribe.

7. Security

Form submissions are stored in private storage that is not publicly accessible, and information is transmitted over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Children

The Site is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International users

Doreh is based in the United States, and information is processed in the United States.

10. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date shows when it last changed.

11. Contact

To exercise your rights or ask a question, email support@doreh.com, use our contact form, or write to Doreh, Inc., 8549 Wilshire Blvd. #2181, Beverly Hills, California 90212.
