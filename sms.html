Doreh Text Messages
Last updated: October 2026

Doreh is an event invitation and RSVP service. We text guests on behalf of the host who invited them, and we text sign-in codes to people who request them.

What we send

  • A host’s invitation, with a link to RSVP
  • RSVP confirmations
  • Event reminders and a reminder if you haven’t replied
  • Day-of updates, such as a time or address change
  • The host’s thank-you note
  • One-time sign-in codes you request

Every event message is about one event from a named host. We never send advertising or promotions by text.

Why you get them

You gave your number to someone you know, and they invited you to their event on Doreh. Before anything is sent, the host must confirm:

Host’s send screen
I know everyone on this list, and they expect to hear from me about this event.
Send invitations

We record each confirmation with the time and the host’s account.

The first text a guest receives
Doreh
Doreh: Emily Carter invited you to her wedding with David Brooks, Sat Jun 13 in Pasadena. RSVP: doreh.com/r/k8F2 You’re getting this because Emily added your number. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.

Sign-in codes are sent only when you enter your own number and ask for a code.
