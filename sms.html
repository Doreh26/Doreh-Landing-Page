Doreh Text Messages
Last updated: November 2026

Doreh is an event invitation and RSVP service. When a host invites you, Doreh texts you one invitation on their behalf. Further texts come only if you sign up for them.

How you sign up

  1. You get on a host’s guest list in one of two ways. You add yourself with the host’s guest list link, giving your own number (example: doreh.com/join/demo). Or the host adds you, after confirming you agreed to get an invitation from them. Doreh then sends you one text with the host’s name, the event and an RSVP link. Reply STOP to that text and you’ll get no more texts from Doreh.
  2. You open the link and RSVP. Your mobile number is your Doreh account; you confirm it with a one-time code, sent by text or, if you prefer, by phone call. See an example at doreh.com/invite/demo.
  3. If you want texts, you check “Text me invitations and updates for my Doreh events.” The box is unchecked to start, and you can RSVP without it. You can also text JOIN to Doreh’s short code.
  4. We text you a welcome. Without signing up, you get no texts beyond the invitation and any sign-in codes you ask for.

How hosts send invitations

Hosts share a guest list link, and guests add themselves with their own number. Doreh tells them, before they give it, that it will text them the host’s invitation.

A host can also add a guest by hand. Doreh texts an invitation only when the host does all three of these:

  1. Confirms that each guest they added agreed to get an invitation from them by text.
  2. Selects each guest one at a time. There is no “select all” and no sending to an uploaded list.
  3. Confirms that Doreh sends each invitation on their behalf, under their name.

Hosts get nothing of value for sending invitations. See the host’s screen at doreh.com/send/demo.

What we send

  • One invitation per event, at the host’s request
  • One-time sign-in codes you ask for (also available by phone call)
  • Your RSVP confirmation
  • Event reminders
  • Day-of updates, such as a time or address change
  • The host’s thank-you note

Every text is about an event you were invited to, from a named host. Only the invitation goes to people who haven’t signed up. Texts use Doreh’s wording; hosts can add short event details but no links, phone numbers or ads. We never send advertising or promotions by text.
